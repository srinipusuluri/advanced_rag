Complete & Partial Exams

To attain the AI Architect Certification requires a passing grade on the complete AI Architect Certification Exam or a passing grade on the partial AI Architect Certification Exam and the attainment of the AI Professional Certification, as explained below.

Complete Exam Details

The complete AI Architect certification exam covers topics from all 5 modules in the AI Architect certification track. Purchase this exam if you've taken the AI Architecture & Design course and would like to pursue certification as an AI Architect, or if you would like to retake this exam to improve your grade.

Because this complete exam encompasses course modules from two certifications, upon passing the exam you will receive official digital certificates and digital certification badges for both the AI Professional and AI Architect accreditations. If you've already achieved the AI Professional certification, then you do not need to take this complete exam. Instead, you can take the partial AI Architect exam.

Partial Exam Details

The partial AI Architect certification exam covers topics from Modules 13, 14 and 15 in the AI Architect certification track.

Purchase this exam if:

  • you've taken the complete or partial AI Architecture & Design course and
  • you would like to pursue certification as an AI Architect and
  • you've already achieved the AI Professional certification.

If you are already a certified AI Professional and you pass this exam, you will receive an official digital certificate and a digital certification badge for the AI Architect accreditation.
